# 00 BRAIN: Piano Improvisation & Audio Routing

## Musical Architecture
- **Piano as Percussion:** The left hand functions as the drummer/bass engine with wide root spacings; the right hand functions as melodic lead.
- **Polyrhythm Bait:** Establish steady 4/4 meter, inject subtle 1.5-beat intentional drift, then snap hard on the downbeat.
- **Core Harmonic Movements:** Open E Major foundations ($E - A - B$) sliding into chromatic mediants and unexpected minor shifts ($Am - Dm - Gm$).
- **Audio Routing:** Multi-channel DAW routing for zero-latency live streams on TikTok/IG.
