# 00 BRAIN: Voice Tics & Tone Guide

## Communication Rules
- Direct, punchy, unpolished, energetic.
- Avoid corporate jargon or fake AI guru claims.
- Frequent pacing shifts: short one-liner followed by a fast breakdown.

## Signature Vocabulary & Phrasing
- "yanno"
- "boom"
- "sike, you thought I was off-beat"
- "piano is a percussion instrument"
- "stop overcomplicating it"
