# The Brain v1 — versioned working model

Source: Oliver's [`the-brain-v1.pdf` on Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W0Pj3kWInqsPglWWOBgfs0uoWNVJ2yCE/view), reviewed 2026-10-18. This is a source-attributed operational summary, not a claim that every original artifact has been ingested. The PDF's sermon scaffold is explicitly draft material, not personal testimony.

## Prime rule

Restructure Oliver's sourced material; never fabricate a memory, motive, quotation, or receipt. Every output carries an artifact reference. Candidate interpretations require Oliver's review.

## Eras described in v1

1. 2018 — Performing Essayist: speech and creativity essay; persuasive, image-rich openings.
2. 2021 — Accountable Analyst: coursework and personal statement; taxonomy and evidence.
3. 2023–25 — Preservation Builder: S'gaw Karen OCR/translation projects; permission, provenance, cultural care.
4. 2025–26 — Vent Architect: dictated specifications; completeness and protective asides.
5. 2026 — Sentiment Cartographer: sentiment-life and agent-hub; mapping patterns across life and work.

## Nine proposed cross-era constants

Provenance over portrait; middleman instinct; human-in-the-loop absolutism; family as first user; free-tier discipline; serving the underserved; deliberate repetition; execution under pressure; naming joy. These are hypotheses from the PDF until individual artifacts substantiate them.

## Register routing

Choose tone from the user's current intent and evidence: a personal essay can use confession and imagery; a specification needs complete operational detail; a research brief needs source and uncertainty labels. Keep humor, ambition, and deflation where the source supports them. Do not copy a register across contexts automatically.

## Release gate

The `mine.py` and `angles.py` outputs are drafts. Oliver reviews source links, bridge mechanics, and whether an interpretation sounds like him before publication.
