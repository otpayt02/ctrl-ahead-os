Narrator: One video title this week promised four huge model updates. GPT-7, Claude REA banned, Gemini Carbon insane, Qwen 4 leaks. I checked all four against what the companies actually said. One is a rumor, one I could not find at all, one is a leak with no product, and one is real but not shipped. Here is the scorecard, so you do not build on a rumor.
Narrator: First, GPT-7 Bel. The whole thing traces to one anonymous post on August 25th. No document, no named source, and it did not even say GPT-7 — that label came later. OpenAI has never used the name Bel in public, and on October 11th its own models page listed GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna. No Bel, no GPT-7. What OpenAI did say, on September 8th, is that it has an internal model significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra. That is true, and it is not the same as saying that model is Bel, or that it is GPT-7. Verdict: unverified.
Narrator: Second, Claude REA banned. I searched for a model called Claude REA, and I did not find one. What I did find is two real bans, and neither is a model. On October 8th, Anthropic updated its usage policy, effective November 12th, to ban sustained, needless cruelty toward Claude — and the main enforcement is Claude ending the conversation, not a model being pulled. And on October 12th, Anthropic said it banned tens of thousands of accounts for trying to copy Claude by distillation. So banned is real, but it is accounts and a policy, not a model called REA.
Narrator: Third, Gemini 4 Carbon. This one is the closest to real, and it is still not a product. Business Insider reported on October 9th that Google staff were testing Carbon inside Google, on its internal coding platform, and one employee said it feels like Opus 5.5 for coding. Feels like is not a benchmark, there is no model card, no price, no release date, and Google has not confirmed it. A checkpoint briefly showed up on LMArena around October 23rd and was pulled. So Carbon is a leak about an internal checkpoint, not a model you can use today.
Narrator: Fourth, Qwen 4. Alibaba said on September 22nd that Qwen 4 is in training. It named tiers — Max, Plus, Flash, and a 27B model — but gave no specs, no date, and as of October 24th there were no Qwen 4 weights on Hugging Face. The thing you can actually look at today is Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next, which Alibaba itself called a preview of the architecture that will underpin Qwen 4. So Qwen 4 is real as a project, not shipped as a model. And Qwen 4 Coder does not exist yet.
Narrator: So if you are picking a model this week, pick from the ones that actually shipped: GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon in its limited form, Qwen 3.8. The other four names are a rumor, a missing model, a leak, and a training run. That is not less interesting — it is just the difference between news and hype, and that difference is the whole point.
