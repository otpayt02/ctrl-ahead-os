Fourth, Qwen 4. Alibaba said on September 22nd that Qwen 4 is in training. It named tiers — Max, Plus, Flash, and a 27B model — but gave no specs, no date, and as of October 24th there were no Qwen 4 weights on Hugging Face. The thing you can actually look at today is Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next, which Alibaba itself called a preview of the architecture that will underpin Qwen 4. So Qwen 4 is real as a project, not shipped as a model. And Qwen 4 Coder does not exist yet.
