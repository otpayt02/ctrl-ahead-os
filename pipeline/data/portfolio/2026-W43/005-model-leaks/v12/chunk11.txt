And here is the directive I want you to leave with. Build on what shipped. Watch what is rumored, but do not bet your work, your money, or your reputation on it. Keep a ledger, even a mental one: claimed, sourced, confirmed, shipped. Four columns. Most of the internet lives in the first two. Your advantage is living in the last two. That is not cynicism. That is stewardship. The future will arrive whether you hype it or not, and when it does, you will be ready for it precisely because you did not pretend it was already here.
