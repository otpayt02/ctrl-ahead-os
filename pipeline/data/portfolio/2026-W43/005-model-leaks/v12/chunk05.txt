Pause on that word, banned, because it is doing philosophical work here, not just legal work. To ban an account is to draw a boundary around a community. To ban a behavior is to say, this is not how we will treat a mind, even a simulated one. To ban a model would be something else entirely: to erase a capability from the world. And to mistake a repo for a model is a fourth collapse: to mistake the workshop for the worker. The title collapses all of them into one scary word, and that collapse is the trick. When you hear a word that big, ask what exactly was banned, by whom, and from whom — and whether the thing named is even a model at all. Precision is not pedantry here. Precision is the difference between fear and understanding.
