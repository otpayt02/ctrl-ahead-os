A leak is a model before it has a name, a price, or a promise. It lives in the gap between what a lab knows and what the public is allowed to know, and that gap is where imagination rushes in. That is not a flaw in you. It is how humans think about power we cannot yet touch. We fill the unknown with the biggest thing we can picture. The discipline is to let the unknown stay unknown for a little longer. A checkpoint is not a product. A feeling is not a benchmark. A training run is not a release. Naming each stage honestly is how you keep your own thinking clean.
