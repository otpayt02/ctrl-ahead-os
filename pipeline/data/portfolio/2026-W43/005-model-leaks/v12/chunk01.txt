Before the evidence, here is the thought I want you to carry through all four names. A rumor is not just a bad fact. It is a mirror. It shows you what you already wanted to be true. When a title promises the next giant model, the next ban, the next leak, it is not only selling news, it is selling certainty in a field that has almost none. The directive is simple, and it is hard: want the truth more than you want the headline. If you can hold that for six minutes, the rest of this video will feel less like a debunk and more like a discipline.
