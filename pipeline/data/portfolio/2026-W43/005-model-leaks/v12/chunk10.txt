One last test before you share anything this week. Ask who benefits if you believe it now, before it is confirmed. If the answer is a channel selling urgency, a seller selling access, or your own desire to be first, slow down. Speed is not insight. Being first to repeat a rumor is not the same as being right, and in a field that moves this fast, the person who waits for confirmation is the one still standing on solid ground when the hype cycle turns.
